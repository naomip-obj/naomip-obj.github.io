01 / PROFILE

Heyy 👋 I'm Naomi.

I turn ideas, products and identities into visual experiences.

I am a 3D artist based in Sicily, specializing in the creation of high-impact photorealistic 3D renders and immersive visual content. I hold a degree in New Technologies of Art from the Academy of Fine Arts of Catania.

My work combines aesthetics, technology and research to create visual content designed to capture attention, communicate clearly and create value for brands, designers and companies.

My approach integrates 3D modeling, advanced lighting, compositing, animation and sound design, with each project shaped around identity, communication goals and visual impact.
